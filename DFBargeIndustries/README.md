[Deepflame] Barge Industries
=================================
Barge Industries allows your Industrial Barges to be used for more than a large storage space and a metal alternative to the wooden barge.

This mod introduces:
- Land Reclamation; Eliminate deep ocean so you can build there.
- Offshore Drilling; A faster way of pumping petrol.
- Offshore Mining; A sure-fire way to collect crushed gold ore.

Of course, this requires the open seas to work, and the barge cannot move while in operation.
You will have to use a boat to transport your goods.
